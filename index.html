EDEN GLOBAL MINISTRIES
This is the official website of Eden Global Ministries

OVERVIEW

An apostolic family, saddled with the responsibility to raise kingdom men from every sphere of life, dominating the kingdoms of the world, establishing the kingdom of our God and His Christ.

MISSION

We raise men through the instrumentality of God's word, demonstrating the power and Spirit of God, showing and demonstrating the tangible love of Jesus though evangelism, outreaches, humanitarian work etc.

OBJECTIVES

  1. To see people raised up, full of the Spirit, with proven character, functioning effectively across society, beyond the four walls of the church building, who can bring about a manifestation of the kingdom of heaven on earth.

  2. To teach God's undiluted word to God's people, demonstrating God's power and Spirit, thereby conforming many to the Christ.

  3. To promote God's love to humanity through outreaches and community services.

Eden Global Ministries is a multifaceted ministry that does not only focus on or is concerned only about the spiritual aspect/part of himanity, but also it's every sphere. That means: As much as the ministry is concerned about the spiritual well-being of people, it is also concerned about everything that pertains to them; education, art's and entertainment, commerce/business, family, politics, media, and so forth. This is why the ministry is has arms that ensures that her major duties to God and to men are fully, duly, and proper fulfilled to the fullest. Below is a list of these arms.


  1. THE EDEN EXPERIENCE

    2. This is the ministry arm that focuses and centres solely on the spiritual and psychological well-being of the people through the instrumentality of the sovereign and undiluted word of God and prayer by the Spirit of God.

  2. NAIOTH INCORPORATED

    3. This is the ministry arm that focuses solely on training, empowering, and raising leaders in every sphere: in churches, schools, industries, sports, arts etc. It hosts a particular meeting aside others tagged THE SONS OF BENJAMIN that trains pastors, church leaders, etc.

  3. BETHEL MINISTRIES

    4. This is the ministry arm that hosts a weekdays online prayer meeting tagged PROPHETIC MERCY PRAYERS(PMP) through which hundreds and thousands are blessed and encounter help from God, and other meetings as the Spirit sanctions.

  4. THE LOVESPREAD FOUNDATION

    5. This is the ministry arm that is focused on the welfare of the people. It is focused solely to provide health support, educational support, community service, and humanitarian/philantropical responsibilities.


Within these above mentioned arms are encapsulated the entirety of the needs of humanity. With the arms functioning at optimal capacity, the society is helped greatly to the degree of grace that God has provided and is still providing. Led by responsible individuals whom grace has elected, these arms function to the glory of God Almighty.

Married to the absolute and beautiful love of his life, the high priest of this great commission, Apostle Gideon Wonah, has by the Holy Spirit of God and support of his dear wife, Mrs.Imelda Gideon Wonah, front lined the visions and still does so with so much passion, faithfulness, and commitment. With such devotion to fostering the kingdom of God he has also by the Spirit taught series upon series of sermons that are more than able to shape the listeners to the perfect image in God's mind for them and to aid them in arriving God's expected end for them. These sermons are freely available across streaming platforms.
